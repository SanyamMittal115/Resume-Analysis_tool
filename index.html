    How it works

    The original Streamlit edition used Hugging Face pipelines for zero-shot role classification, neural summarization and NER skill detection. This Vercel edition keeps the same output categories while running deterministic browser analysis so the public deployment stays responsive and your resume remains on your device.

    The professional summary is synthesized from detected experience, education, quantified impact and capabilities instead of copying the first lines of the resume.
